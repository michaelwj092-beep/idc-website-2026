# Inspire Designs Collective — Official Website

The official company website for Inspire Designs Collective, a church website design agency that builds custom websites for churches and ministries.

## About

Inspire Designs Collective exists to take the weight of web design off ministry teams so they can stay focused on what they were called to do. We bring over twelve years of real ministry experience alongside our design work.

## What This Site Includes

- **Homepage** — Brand introduction, client system overview, portfolio preview, and process summary
- **Why Us?** — The story behind IDC and why ministry experience changes everything about how we build
- **Plans & Pricing** — Three tiered pricing plans built for ministries of every size
- **Our Work** — Case studies for Baggage Claim, Cal Industries, and Grace Community Church concept site
- **How It Works** — Six step process walkthrough with interactive Relay and Pinpoint demos
- **Contact** — Lead capture form wired to Supabase CRM with email notification via Resend

## Client Tools

- **Relay** — Client portal mockup showing the monthly management dashboard experience
- **Pinpoint** — Interactive revision tool with guided 11 step tutorial built directly into the demo

## Tech Stack

- Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Supabase for contact form lead capture
- Resend for email notifications
- Netlify for hosting and deployment

## Deployment

Deployed via Netlify with automatic deploys on push to main branch.

Live site: https://inspiredesignscollective.com

## Notes

- Contact form submissions are captured in Supabase and trigger an email notification to hello@inspiredesignscollective.com
- config.js holds Supabase credentials and must be configured locally before the contact form will function
- The Pinpoint demo at mockup/concept-church-pinpoint.html includes a full guided tutorial system
