## 01 — THE BRAND

Inspire Designs Collective provides graphic creation, media, website management, and marketing services built on technical precision, intentional craft, direct collaboration, and transparent communication. We partner with churches and ministries to equip leaders and help them fulfill their God-given calling.

### What We Do
- Graphic Design — Visual assets built to serve the message, not just decorate it
- Media — Media produced with purpose, from concept through final delivery
- Website Management — Sites built and maintained alongside the leaders who depend on them
- Marketing Services — Campaigns grounded in clear timelines and honest updates

### Our Principles
**Intentional — Words**
Verbiage is chosen with the same care as the graphics we design. Every word and every client interaction is deliberate, because how we communicate reflects on the ministries we serve.

**Faithful — Actions**
We do what we say we'll do. Ministry leaders hand us the technical, detail-heavy work with confidence, because faithful follow-through is what we stake our name on.

**Timely — Deadlines**
Deadlines aren't a suggestion, they're a commitment. Concise, on-time delivery means pastors and leaders can plan around us and stay focused on their people.

### Policies & Standards
**Brand & Marketing Approval Policy**
No marketing collateral, social media graphic, ad campaign, print material, or visual asset may be published, distributed, or printed without explicit approval from both owners: Michael Johnson and Calvin Johnson.

**Visual Quality Standard**
Plain, solid, or uninspired backgrounds are strictly prohibited for outward-facing marketing assets. Backgrounds must be intentional, visually engaging, and aligned with our brand aesthetic.

## 02 — LOGO SYSTEM

The logo is built from three parts: the circular icon topped with a diamond, the wordmark, and horizontal versions for light and dark backgrounds.

### Logo Versions
- **Icon / Brand Mark** — For small-scale use including avatars, favicons, and standalone accents
- **Primary Logo** — For light backgrounds, white paper, and light surface media
- **Secondary Logo** — For dark backgrounds, dark screens, and presentation covers

Approved formats: full color logo with icon and wordmark, full color icon only, or wordmark only when the icon is not appropriate.

### Protection & Scale
- Icon minimum: 24px digital / 10mm print
- Full logo minimum width: 120px digital / 30mm print
- Favicon exception: 16px
- Clear space: the width of the diamond on every side
- Never shrink the mark so far that COLLECTIVE becomes illegible

### Color & Backgrounds
- Light backgrounds: use the full color logo on white, Light Gray, or clean neutral backgrounds
- Dark backgrounds: use the white wordmark version
- Never place the logo over photographs, patterns, or colors that reduce contrast or readability

### What Not To Do
- No drop shadows or glow effects
- No recoloring outside approved treatments
- No stretching, squishing, or distorting
- No rotating
- No rearranging icon and wordmark spacing
- No setting the wordmark in another typeface

## 05 — VOICE & TONE

We speak with the confidence of people who've done the work, and real respect for the leaders we serve. Our voice is direct, warm, intentional, collaborative, technically sound, and human.

### How We Speak
**DO:**
- "Here's the draft for Sunday's graphic. Happy to adjust before it's final."
- "The new page layout will help visitors find service times faster. Here's why I'd recommend it."
- "Your approval is needed by Thursday to keep the print timeline on track."

**DON'T:**
- "I'll get to it this week."
- "Made some changes, take a look."
- "Just need your approval when you get a chance."

Specific, collaborative, and clear: it respects the other person's time and builds trust.

### Communication Style
**Reach for:** specificity, honesty, collaboration, warmth
**Avoid:** "just", "simply", "obviously", vague buzzwords

## 06 — AI INTEGRATIONS

### Hard Stops — Non-Negotiable
1. **Source the logo. Don't recreate it.** Pull the logo file directly from the brand/logos/ folder. Never redraw, regenerate, or reinterpret the mark.
2. **Public Sans is mandatory.** Check availability first, fetch from Google Fonts if needed, never substitute without explicit approval.
3. **Drafting is not publishing.** No marketing collateral, social graphic, ad, or print material may be published or distributed without explicit approval from both owners, Michael Johnson and Calvin Johnson.

### Background System

**Three approved background looks:**

**BLUE**
Base: near-black navy #0A0F1C. Glow: Electric Blue halo, lighter blue-white core.

**CHARCOAL**
Base: brand Charcoal #1A1A1A. Two variants: Quiet (neutral grey glow) or Accent (single brand hue glow).

**LIGHT**
Base: White or Light Gray. Glow: soft whisper pulling from the full gradient, capped low.

### Background Construction Rules
1. One glow source only — positioned off-center at a corner or edge, never centered or symmetric
2. Two layers per glow, same hue only — soft outer halo plus brighter inner core
3. Brand colors only — only the four documented brand colors plus Charcoal and White
4. Always apply subtle grain — a flat noiseless gradient reads as cheap
5. Never pure black — always use Charcoal #1A1A1A or the Blue look's navy #0A0F1C
6. Glow is lighting, not decoration — never under body text, never reduces legibility
7. Everything outside the glow stays flat
8. Never stretch or rotate to a new shape — regenerate at correct dimensions instead

### Background Creative Freedom
- Position: whichever corner has the least content
- Size: glow footprint roughly 20-45% of canvas
- Canvas: size and aspect ratio can change to fit the format
- Opacity: within the documented range for each look
- Charcoal hue: Quiet (neutral grey) or Accent (single brand hue), pick one per piece

### Header Component
- Full lockup only (icon plus wordmark) — never icon-only in a header
- Top accent bar: full width, thin, using the brand gradient at 135 degrees
- Right-aligned: document title and website
- Use for: multi-page documents, detailed one-pagers, proposals
