WHY US?

Many talented designers can build a beautiful website.

At Inspire Designs Collective we bring something more. Alongside great design we bring over twelve years of real ministry experience. We have been in the rooms where ministry happens. We understand the pace, the pressure, and the weight your team carries. Ministry leaders wear a lot of hats. Your website does not have to be one of them. That is what we are here for.

Most church websites are not bad because churches do not care.

They are bad because nobody knew how to ask for what they actually wanted. That gap between what a pastor envisions and what ends up on the screen is where most website projects fall apart. We built Inspire Designs to live in that gap.

12 YEARS

Over a decade in ministry taught us one thing.

Our team has spent over twelve years working in and around churches and ministries. Not as an outsider looking in, but inside the rooms where the decisions get made. We built Inspire Designs around what we learned there. Not to be the flashiest agency, but to be the most useful one.

THE GAP

Nobody goes into ministry to manage a website.

We know how many pastors think. We know what they need and we know how hard it is for them to communicate that when they are thinking about Sunday messages, small group leader meetings, staff meetings, upcoming events, ministry area responsibilities, and the person who walked in midweek for counseling that took two hours of their afternoon. All of those things are happening at once. And somewhere in the middle of all of it, a pastor is supposed to figure out how to communicate their website vision to a designer who has never planned a Sunday service, sat in a staff meeting, or led a ministry area.

INSPIRE

The name Inspire Designs was not chosen by accident.

We exist to take the frustrations, the confusion, the websites that nobody wants to touch, and turn them into something that actually inspires. Inspiring ideas. Inspiring designs. A digital presence your congregation is proud of and your community can connect with. We will build for anyone who needs a great website. But churches and ministries have a soft spot in our heart. This is the world we know.
This is the work we love.
